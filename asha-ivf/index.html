Should we warm one more embryo?

After an embryo is warmed, we photograph it during recovery. The AI grades that photo from 0 to 10 and shows the full history of the embryo, from biopsy to pre-transfer, so the team can decide whether to transfer now or thaw another.

  2. Photo during recovery

  4. Transfer one, or warm one more?
